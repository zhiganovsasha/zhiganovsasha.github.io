# Market Data Research cover brief

## Purpose and placement

- Purpose: project-card cover for the Market Data Research case study, showing a web-scale scraping and filtering pipeline that turns millions of raw catalog records into verified market analytics.
- Intended page and component: `/development` (`src/pages/development/index.astro`, `ProjectCard.astro`).
- Approximate rendered width: 320 to 440 CSS pixels on desktop grid (`minmax(320px, 1fr)`), up to 100vw on mobile (`widths={[400, 720]}`).
- Aspect ratio: 16:9, `object-fit: cover`.
- Final pixel dimensions: 1440 by 810 pixels.
- Safe area: keep the outer 10 percent quiet so `object-fit: cover` and hover `scale(1.02)` never clip the focal forms.
- Object count: one dominant central mechanism and three to four bold supporting forms.
