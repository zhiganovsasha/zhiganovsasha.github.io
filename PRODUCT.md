# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro with static output. The site must work on GitHub Pages, have no runtime
dependency on GitHub, and remain portable to a regular domain and hosting.

## Users

The primary user is a person making a hiring decision about Alexander for an
international role, with the UAE as the first relocation market. They need to
understand which side of his practice is relevant to the role and then inspect
credible proof without reconstructing his contribution from scattered sources.

## Product Purpose

The site presents Alexander Zhiganov as a whole practitioner and routes visitors
to the part of his work that matches their interest. The home page asks `What
would you like to explore?` rather than acting as a selected-work portfolio.

Each destination has its own immediate message. The first impression on AI
Development must prove that Alexander builds real working products with AI. Art,
education, speaking, and the blog will later establish their own page-specific
messages instead of inheriting a generic claim from the home page.

Success means that a hiring decision-maker can quickly identify the relevant
practice, understand Alexander's role and way of working, inspect real evidence,
and reach the approved contact or CV action.

## Positioning

Public name: `Alexander Zhiganov`.

Primary professional title: `Creative Technologist`.

Core statements:

- `I turn research into ideas, and ideas into working products.`
- `I build with AI already, as a habit rather than an experiment.`
- `Working at the intersection of art, technology, and AI.`

The story must show a repeatable mechanism: research becomes ideas, decisions,
products, actions, and value. Alexander can develop a concept, assemble a team,
organize the process, and bring the result to a client or a working product. AI
agents are an ordinary production and development environment, not an isolated
experiment.

The general positioning must not use `Creative Producer`, `New Media`, or
`Multimedia`, and must not make Alexander look like a freelancer, an advertising
specialist, or a mobile advertising campaign specialist.

## Operating Context

The first version is English-only, light-theme, static, and responsive for
desktop and mobile. It will initially be published through GitHub Pages only
after separate owner approval.

The first version contains the home page, `/development`, four detailed product
cases, and AI Video Production inside `/development`. Art Projects, Education &
Speaking, and Blog are visible directions but remain unavailable until later.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home routes visitors to AI Development, Art Projects, Education & Speaking,
  and Blog.
- AI Development is active in the first version; the other three directions use
  accessible unavailable-state modal behavior without changing the URL.
- Primary navigation contains Home, AI Development, and Contact.
- The first version has no language or theme switcher.
- CV choices are AI Developer, Creative Director, and Educator & Speaker; only AI
  Developer is initially active.
- Full project stacks belong inside case studies. `My AI toolkit` stays compact.
- Project claims, roles, results, clients, integrations, and production status
  must be supported by the approved specification and source repositories.
- Client and third-party data must never appear in public demonstrations.
- The medium dot character is not used as a separator.
- Publication, deployment, Git operations, and changes to external services each
  require their own explicit approval.

## Brand Commitments

The binding color palette is white, black, and a dark restrained burgundy. Bright
signal red is not used. The exact proportions, typography, motion language, and
component system remain open design decisions.

The home hero uses the portrait photograph at
`C:\Users\1234\Pictures\userpic.jpg`; the source file remains
unchanged and the site receives separate optimized versions. The public
portrait must use those literal source pixels. The face, hair, skin, pose, and
clothing must not be generated, redrawn, beautified, retouched, color-edited, or
reinterpreted. Layout may only scale, crop, and position the portrait.

The visual system must avoid typical AI design, AI slop, vibe-coding aesthetics,
graphic-designer portfolio cues, art-gallery cues, stereotypical creative-coding
elements, and decorative generative patterns. Serial labels such as
`01 / PROFILE` and numbered navigation rows such as `01`, `02`, `03`, `04` are
not used. Attention must come from functional minimalism, portrait composition,
typographic hierarchy, and purposeful motion.

## Product Principles

1. Let the visitor choose the relevant side of Alexander's practice before
   presenting detailed evidence.
2. Give every destination a page-specific first impression and proof sequence.
3. Show real products, decisions, processes, and outcomes instead of generic
   self-description.
4. Present AI as an integrated working method while keeping Alexander's judgment,
   authorship, and ability to deliver visible.
5. Preserve the distinction between production systems, substantial work in
   progress, local tools, and future directions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, and
responsive reading on desktop and mobile. Unavailable cards must work by hover
and keyboard on desktop and by tap on mobile, with an accessible modal and no URL
change.
